- A token that a stray ESC, `ESC (`, `ESC [` or an unterminated OSC takes part of while a second escape splits it, and a token split across two lines, are redacted in the local CI log, its tail and text artifacts. Before, the cleanup left pieces that no pattern matched, and they were kept as written. A binary artifact holding one is not copied.
- An environment value is redacted however escapes, controls and line breaks split it, with up to 1024 of them between two of its characters; docs/state.md names the one shape left, a value that runs on from another secret and needs an escape's own bytes read as its characters. Token shapes are found as well as the cleanup's views allow; docs/state.md lists the known misses.
