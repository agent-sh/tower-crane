- `tower-crane metrics [--since 7d|ISO] [--until ISO] [--by day] [--claimed-after ISO]` reports the tasks merged in a window with their whole history: merges per day, gate runs per merged task by gate, reworks by cause and the share Tower Crane caused, tokens per merged task by role, rung and harness with the entries that have no tokens counted apart, lead-time phases with the waiting share, and submits per task with first-pass time. `--claimed-after` limits every number to tasks first claimed at or after a time and says how many it left out. It reads only the event log, without identity, lease or lock, and writes nothing; `--json` names each definition.
- `rework` events record `cause` (conflict_base, conflict_parent, stack_sync, coverage_gap, base_failure, timeout, infrastructure, head_check, tests, clean, ci, review or manual). `head check` events record `started_at`, when the suite started.
