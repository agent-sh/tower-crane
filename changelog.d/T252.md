- Gate commands no longer leave processes behind on Linux. A test fixture's detached process in a new session escaped the gate's process-group kill and kept running after the gate removed its directory. The gate now marks each run's environment and also stops marked processes and the descendants of its group.
- The slow stub suite in `test/automation.test.js` records its pid, exits when its fixture is gone or after four minutes, and teardown releases it before removing the fixture.
- Gate commands run with `gc.autoDetach=false`, appended to any `GIT_CONFIG_*` entries already set, so stopping a gate never kills a detached `git gc` mid-write.
