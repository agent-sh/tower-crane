- Review evidence counts toward the review gate only when its agent is a reviewer `spawn` dispatched for that task at that sha and revision, or the owner. Before, any name but the submitter's counted, so an orchestrator or an unsandboxed worker could record an ok review under a made-up name and accept on it. Other review evidence stays in the record, marked `(does not count)`, and the gate names its agent.
- The board ledger marks such review evidence `not a spawned reviewer`. It no longer stops `accept` from dispatching the real reviewer, and failed reviews by such names no longer raise the review rung or reach a rework worker as findings.
