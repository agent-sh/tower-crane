- Spawn no longer fails when Windows briefly refuses to rename the freshly copied skill store into `skills/<key>/` (EPERM while a scanner or the indexer holds the new files). The rename is retried for up to 2 s, as state writes are. A store another dispatch published first still counts as written, and the temporary copy is removed.
- The same retry covers the rename that keeps a failed start's supervisor log, so the startup failure is reported instead of EPERM, and the broker's move of an agent's pack into its push cache.
