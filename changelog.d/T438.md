- Main push CI failures at the current base head share one inbox item, with failing jobs and test names. Followers refresh main CI without `--inbox`.
- Where base push CI has run, publishing and merging a release PR require its latest completed verdict to be green, including when the owner already approved publication. Cancelled runs stay blocking while a newer push is pending.
- Older-head push failures remain actionable while newer CI is pending or filtered out. Neutral and skipped runs are green; projects without base-push history retain their publication flow.
- Completed verdicts remain valid during reruns. Missing workflows are checked through their own base-push history, covering managed CI beyond the shared run listing.
