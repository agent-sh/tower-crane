- `task landed ID --pr N [--sha MERGE_SHA] --reason R` records a task whose PR the orchestrator merged on GitHub outside Tower Crane, after its own review and green checks. It reads the PR once, verifies the merge into the project base and the task's branch or PR, and accepts the task as `accepted (native)` with a `landed` record (merge commit, merged_at, merged_by, check conclusions at the head, reason). No gate reruns and no review is dispatched; dependents unblock, a claim is released and the worktree is retired with its local work saved. Accepted, cancelled and hotfix tasks, owner asks, running workers and PRs of another branch or task are refused.
- `metrics` counts native landings as merges and reports them apart (`tower_crane`, `native_landed`, also per day), and leaves them out of the per-task gate, rework, token, lead-time and submit numbers unless `--include-native`.
- A reworked task cannot be landed again with the PR or merge commit an earlier landing of it used, and it shows `accepted (native)` only while that landing is its current acceptance.
