- After a failed independent review, rework reviews carry its blockers and review only the diff since its SHA. A requirements edit, rewritten history, base update or a reviewed SHA that no longer exists resets the review scope. A narrowed re-review keeps the risk and size of the whole change and never runs below the failed review's rung or level. A defect the new diff causes blocks even in unchanged code; other newly noticed findings on already reviewed work are follow-ups unless they are security or data-loss defects.
