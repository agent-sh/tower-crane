- Owner identity needs a process the owner runs. `--agent owner` or `TOWER_CRANE_AGENT=owner` is refused when `TOWER_CRANE_AGENT` names anyone else (an orchestrator or a worker that lost `TOWER_CRANE_TASK` could act as owner), and when stdin or stdout is not a terminal unless `TOWER_CRANE_OWNER_KEY` holds the owner key. `tower-crane owner-key` creates the key beside the user file; every sandbox hides it and no child process inherits the variable. Every path claude's sandbox hides from Bash is also denied to claude's Read, Grep and Glob tools, which answer to permission rules instead of the sandbox.
