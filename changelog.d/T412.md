- The `wait` host refills worker slots on its own path instead of after its event reactions, so free slots fill while a gate reaction runs the full suite. Refill runs at startup, on every state change, on the fallback tick and after each reaction pass, one at a time per host, and skips a task whose own gate reaction is still live. A wait ends only once neither its reactions nor its refill are running, and the refill after a reaction pass still runs when the wait's deadline came during the pass.
- An ending `gates.rework_hold_min` hold dispatches on a timer set to the earliest hold end, with no other event.
- `status` names why automatic refill leaves each ready task undispatched: `not dispatched: T2 worker limit reached (3 of 3: T1, T4, T5)`, a rework hold until a time, a running gate reaction, no brief, higher-ranked tasks taking the free slots, the dispatch lock's holder, or the host governor's latest worker deferral. JSON has `not_dispatched`.
