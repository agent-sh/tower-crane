- Accepting or cancelling a task archives its scratch to `archive/<task>/` in the state directory before the scratch is removed. The manifest lists each file's path, size and SHA-256. Text gets the gate log redaction, files over 8 MiB keep their head and a truncation notice, and the archive stops at 64 MiB. Tool caches are left out. Archives are kept for `archive.retention_days` (default 30, operational).
- A scratch path cited by the PR body, the task's notes or evidence, or a decision is archived whole. Accept refuses and names a cited path that is missing, not a regular file or over 32 MiB.
- `tower-crane task archive ID` lists an archive. `--into T` copies files into T's scratch and records `archive` evidence on T. The orchestrator copies into any open task; T's worker copies only from an archive granted to T with `--grant T`.
