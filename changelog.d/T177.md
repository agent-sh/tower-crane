- A failed `tests`, `clean` or `ci` gate at a submitted head no longer waits for an operator. A timeout, a runner killed by a signal or a CI run that timed out runs the gate once more at the same head. Any other failure, or a retry that fails too, sends the task to rework with the gate's summary: the failing test names and the output tail. A confirmed quality failure on a task with a tier range keeps its escalation climb.
- A gate that could not run, such as a missing pinned command or a change with no test, stays submitted for the owner to fix or waive. `gates retry` remains for an operator who fixed the environment behind a failed gate.
- A test or cleanup command killed by a signal is recorded as `runner_crash`, not as a confirmed quality failure, so a crash no longer climbs a rung. A shell reports the signal as exit 128 plus its number, and that exit counts the same.
