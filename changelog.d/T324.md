- A local CI run keeps its full stdout and stderr, redacted, in `logs/ci-<task>-<sha>-<unique>/output.log` under the state directory, and records the path in `local_ci.log` on its evidence. A failed run also records `local_ci.output_tail`, the last 200 lines and at most 16384 characters, and its summary names the full log. Before, only the last 20 lines of stderr or stdout survived, and ginza's failing step was not among them.
- `ci.local.artifacts` (globs in the gate worktree) and `ci.local.artifact_pattern` (a regular expression for paths the command prints, such as `^Local check receipt: (.+)$`) name files that `check ci` copies out of the gate worktree and its scratch before removal. Evidence lists them in `local_ci.artifacts`. At most 100 files and 64 MiB per run.
- Gate worktrees and scratch now default to `tower-crane/gates` under the user's cache directory instead of the OS temporary directory, which is often a RAM-backed tmpfs. ginza's local CI failed under `/tmp` because its SSD campaign guard refuses dry media under `/tmp` or on tmpfs. `gates.tmp_root` and `TOWER_CRANE_TMP` still override it, and `status` names the default's source `cache`.
