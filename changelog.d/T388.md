- Reading a binary local CI artifact across line breaks no longer copies the whole file without its line breaks. The join reads 64K-character windows, and the spans found are kept in typed arrays. On a 64 MiB file, 1.6M line-split tokens peaked at 818 MiB and `sk-ab` then `cd` lines at 1.4 GiB; both now stay under 450 MiB. 1.6M NUL-separated tokens drop from 707 to 507 MiB and 2.5M environment values from 768 to 521 MiB.
