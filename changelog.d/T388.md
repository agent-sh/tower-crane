- Reading a binary local CI artifact across line breaks no longer copies the whole file without its line breaks. The join reads 64K-character windows, and the spans found are kept in typed arrays. On a 64 MiB file, 1.6M line-split tokens peaked at 816 MiB and `sk-ab` then `cd` lines at 1.4 GiB; both now stay under 512 MiB.
