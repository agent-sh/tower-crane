- A binary local CI artifact, one with a NUL byte or invalid UTF-8, is redacted at the byte level. Environment secrets, matched by their UTF-8 bytes, and token shapes become ASCII `[redacted:NAME]` markers, and every other byte stays as written. Before, such a file was kept unredacted.
- A binary artifact is not copied when a secret shows that exact byte matches cannot remove: in UTF-16 text read either way and at either byte alignment, or once escapes and controls are removed. Its skipped entry names the view.
- Restoring redaction markers takes one pass. Before, a text with many `[redacted:...]` placeholders took quadratic time: 1 MiB of them took minutes.
