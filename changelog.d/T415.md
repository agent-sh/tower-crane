- Containment cleans up after itself. A scope the user manager leaves failed, as an OOM does, is reset once it settles, after its `Result` is read. Report directories carry the supervisor's pid, so the first contained launch of each process removes the directories and failed scopes of supervisors that were killed.
- Default caps follow the role. A spawned orchestrator holds its workers, reviewers and gates in its own scope, so it gets the whole half of the budget the per-launch defaults split. A reviewer gets one agent share plus one gate share for its test probes. `project show` prints both. Explicit caps still apply unchanged to every role.
- A caller already inside its own capped or delegated cgroup keeps its attempts inside it: nested when the fence delegates `memory` and `pids`, otherwise under the watchdog in place, never in a sibling scope.
- `init`, `project set` and `ladder set` warn when `scope` sets `TasksMax`, `MemoryMax`, `MemorySwapMax`, `OOMPolicy`, `Delegate` or `Slice`, which containment replaces. Every launch in a containment scope gets `TOWER_CRANE_SCOPED=1`.
