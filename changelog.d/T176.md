- Technical decisions belong to the orchestrator by default. `decision_delegation.orchestrator_technical` is true when absent. `ask --question` takes a required `--kind` (`technical`, `spend`, `credential` or `setting`): technical and escalated technical questions without another owner restriction are the orchestrator's under project delegation; approval requests and spend, credential, setting, crisis or `--owner-required` questions stay with the owner unless the owner lifts the restriction. Escalated question answers authorize no command. Open decisions opened before kinds are technical unless an approval request or reason keeps them with the owner, so they are answerable without re-asking. The owner lifts a kind or reason with `decision delegate --technical true`.
