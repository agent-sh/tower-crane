- Technical decisions belong to the orchestrator by default. `decision_delegation.orchestrator_technical` is true when absent, and every question is technical unless it names an owner-required setting, carries `ask --owner-required REASON`, or asks for spend or credentials, which stay with the owner without a marker. Owner-only decisions refuse every other answer, and open technical decisions become answerable without re-asking. The owner keeps technical decisions by setting `orchestrator_technical` to false, and clears a spend, credential or `--owner-required` reason that does not fit with `decision delegate --technical true`.
