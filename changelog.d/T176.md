- Technical decisions belong to the orchestrator by default. `decision_delegation.orchestrator_technical` is true when absent. `ask --question` takes a required `--kind` (`technical`, `spend`, `credential` or `setting`): only a technical question is the orchestrator's, while spend, credential and setting questions, and questions with an owner-required setting or `--owner-required` reason, stay with the owner. Open technical decisions stay answerable without re-asking, and the owner clears a kind or reason with `decision delegate --technical true`.
