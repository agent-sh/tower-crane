- A token whose prefix a line break splits, such as `g` at a line's end and `hp_...` on the next line, is redacted in the local CI log, its tail and text artifacts, and a binary artifact holding one is not copied. A break inside a prefix joins when at least two of the prefix's characters come before it or three after it, so `0.3 s` then a line `K-long_async` stays text. Such a token is not put back over an environment value or another secret it overlaps, one a gentle view recovers included, and split tokens next to each other go back apart.
