- A spawn supervisor runs only its own task's reactions after its agent exits, then exits. It no longer drains other tasks' queued gates or merges the rest of the line; when it stops at another task's head it releases the line with a queued `automation reconcile` handoff, which the wait host runs.
- Reaction and merge queue receipts, gate events, `accept` and `spawn` record the tool that wrote them as `tool: {sha, version, committed}`. An executor whose tool is behind the newest one recorded queues its reactions for a current executor, and its gates, accepts, merges, dispatches and refill refuse.
- `status` lists live executors (reactions, the merge queue holder and spawn supervisors) with their tool version and age, and marks one behind the project's engine tool `STALE`.
