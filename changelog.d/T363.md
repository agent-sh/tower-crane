- `spawn --wait` and the supervisor no longer wait forever when a process the agent started keeps the harness output open after the harness exits. Five seconds after the harness and its group have stopped, the supervisor stops the processes it can trace to the harness (Linux by an environment marker that nested launches do not inherit, Windows by a process snapshot checked against each pid's creation time), closes the pipes two seconds later, keeps the output it read, stops renewing the lease and records `output_held` on `spawn exit`.
