- Sandboxed claude commands reach the network again. claude makes its proxy bridge sockets in its `TMPDIR`, and with the caller's under the home, which the sandbox hides, every https request from a command failed with `Proxy CONNECT aborted`. A sandboxed claude agent now gets a temp directory of its own that its sandbox reads and writes, as codex agents do.
- The claude sandbox allows an explicit list of hosts in place of every host: GitHub's, `registry.npmjs.org` and the hosts of the repository's remotes. The new `sandbox.domains` setting (project and rung, owner-required) adds more, and `*` opts back in to every host. A pattern claude's schema does not take is refused at `project set`, `ladder set` and spawn.
