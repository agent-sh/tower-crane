- Sandboxed claude commands reach the network again. claude makes its proxy bridge sockets in its `TMPDIR`, and with the caller's under the home, which the sandbox hides, every https request from a command failed with `Proxy CONNECT aborted`. A sandboxed claude agent now gets a temp directory of its own that its sandbox reads and writes, as codex agents do. Under a cache root too deep for the sockets, spawn prints a notice that the agent keeps the caller's `TMPDIR`.
- The claude sandbox allows an explicit list of hosts in place of every host: GitHub's and `registry.npmjs.org`. The new `sandbox.domains` setting (project and rung, owner-required) adds more, and `*` opts back in to every host. A repository's remote adds no host, so a new remote cannot widen egress without the owner; spawn names each remote host and port the list leaves out, loopback and private addresses marked, in a notice and in the `spawn` event. A pattern claude's schema does not take (`github.com:080`, an IPv6 literal without brackets) or a wildcard over a shared suffix (`*.co.uk`, `*.github.io`) is refused at `project set`, `ladder set` and spawn.
