- `status`, `inbox` and the orchestrator's observer (`wait --observe`, `--inbox`) read the task rows, the events of the tasks they look at and the few commands they list, through indexes. None of them reads the whole event log. On a copy of the live state, `status` p95 went from about 1 s to about 0.2 s and `inbox` from about 1 s to about 0.27 s.
- The gate queue reads its reaction receipts, queue entries, requests and acceptances through indexes, and the merge queue reads its newest entries first. Drain order and the queue that `status` and `inbox` show are unchanged.
- `state.db` has four more indexes: `(cmd, task, pos)`, the agent an event's detail names, and the source and phase of reaction receipts. The first command that can write a database built before them adds them; until then reads return the same rows, more slowly.
