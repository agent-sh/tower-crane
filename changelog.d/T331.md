- The broker's own `ls-remote`, fetch and push to the remote no longer hold the project's cache lock forever when the remote stops answering, as over a network that drops without a reset. Each runs with git's progress and is stopped, with the ssh it started, after 10 minutes without output or an hour in all, the limits of an agent's pack. The agent's push or fetch fails with the reason, and the lock is free for the project's other pushes and fetches.
