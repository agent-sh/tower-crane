- Stacked children merge and push a parent's resubmitted head before worker dispatch or gates. Untouched children adopt the new head directly, preserving a parent's rewritten history. Clean refreshes update the recorded parent head and emit `stack sync`; conflicts leave the branch unchanged and dispatch the worker with the parent sha and conflicting files. Gates wait for a child submission containing the current parent head. Merged and retiring parents keep their existing lifecycle checks, including PR-base validation before retirement and dependency block reasons before dispatch.
