- Stacked children merge and push a parent's resubmitted head before worker or reviewer dispatch, acceptance, or software gates. Cached receipts cannot authorize a child whose submitted head lacks the latest parent submission. Untouched children adopt the new head directly, using an exact-head lease to recover a published branch after a parent rewrite. A rejected lease preserves remote work and records a note. Children with their own commits are restacked across parent rewrites rather than merging dropped history. Linked refresh resumes ordinary sync after an inapplicable or recoverably failed parent refresh. Clean refreshes update the recorded parent head and emit `stack sync`; conflicts leave the branch unchanged and dispatch the worker with the parent sha and conflicting files. Gates require a child submission containing the parent's latest submitted head, including while that parent is in rework or has an active claim. Merged and retiring parents keep their existing lifecycle checks, including PR-base validation before retirement and dependency block reasons before dispatch.
- The retry-home lifecycle test waits for the monitor's retry receipt instead of treating a harness readiness marker as proof of publication. Regression cases cover both publication orders with the platform launch and launch handshake.
