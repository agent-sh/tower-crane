- Stacked children merge and push a parent's resubmitted head before worker dispatch or gates. Untouched children adopt the new head directly, preserving a parent's rewritten history. Clean refreshes update the recorded parent head and emit `stack sync`; conflicts leave the branch unchanged and dispatch the worker with the parent sha and conflicting files. Gates require a child submission containing the parent's latest submitted head, including while that parent is in rework or has an active claim. Merged and retiring parents keep their existing lifecycle checks, including PR-base validation before retirement and dependency block reasons before dispatch.
