- `submit --pr` checks the PR's CI and revuto at the submitted head before it records the head. Pending checks refuse with the job names and `wait for CI`; a failing job refuses with its name and the first failing lines of its log; revuto's open inline findings print with file, line and body. Capped reviews do not block. Before, a worker could submit a head whose Windows jobs were already red on its PR, and the gate and rework round ran after.
- A PR that GitHub reports CONFLICTING does not block the submit: GitHub runs no checks on it, so submit says CI was not checked and the orchestrator sends the conflict back as rework. A mergeability GitHub has not computed yet counts as pending.
- `submit --force-ci-pending` submits past pending checks only when the brief has the line `force-ci-pending: allowed`. Failing checks always refuse.
- A harness whose gh policy refuses `gh api` (no OS sandbox) cannot read the checks at submit. Its submit prints that the head was not checked and goes ahead; the orchestrator's `check ci` still judges the head before merge.
- The worker role says to push, wait for CI, fix and then submit.
