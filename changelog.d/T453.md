- A spawned worker owns its PR until it is green. After submit it stays in its session and waits with `wait --own --task ID`; a rework at its head (a failed tests, clean or ci gate, a failed review, a merge conflict) hands the task back to it, claimed, with the rework's full reason as a message, instead of dispatching a fresh worker. The revision and rework notes advance as before. It exits when the task merges, when it is interrupted, or after `limits.owner_idle_minutes` (default 180) with no event for its task; a later failure then dispatches a new worker as before.
- Waiting owners hold no worker slot and have their own cap, `limits.owners` (default twice `limits.workers`); a submit past it tells the worker to exit. A handback takes its claim whatever the worker limit says. The host governor sizes a waiting owner by its measured memory, not the worker's expected peak.
- `project set --owners N` and `--owner-idle-minutes MIN` set both limits. `interrupt ID` on a submitted task stops its waiting owner.
