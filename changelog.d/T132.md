- `tower-crane decision withdraw DID --reason R` closes an open decision that became moot. The agent that opened it or the owner can withdraw it while no one has answered it, without an owner answer. The decision leaves the open list in `status` and `decisions --open`, the `decision-withdrawn` event records the reason, and the decision stays in history. Blocked tasks get the reason in their notes. Answered or already withdrawn decisions are refused. A sandboxed worker or reviewer can run it through the state broker, under the same asker-only check.
