- Headless workers and reviewers wait for CI and tests in the foreground with a bounded `tower-crane wait --timeout`, not in a background job; the work and review skills say so.
- The Stop hook holds an unsubmitted worker once when it ends with a background Bash job or no final report. A stop with a background job still pending sends the orchestrator no "stopped without submit" note, so a worker that is still working does not read as dead.
