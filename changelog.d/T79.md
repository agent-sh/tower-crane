- The board is rebuilt on its own design system (docs/design.md, from T70's docs/human-experience.md): a status sentence that is also the tab title, five rooms at real paths (`/`, `/review`, `/plan`, `/spend`, `/history`, with Settings at `/settings`) where the URL decides the room, a queue in two tiers (Now and Your turn) that holds decisions, approvals drawn as sentences, owner tasks, messages, runaways, stuck claims and budget alarms, a floor of agent rows with lease and usage drawn as lengths and live, stale or unknown usage in words, a Review room with the reviewer's finding and gate receipts that say what each pass proved, and a spend line with burn rate, projection and the top spender. Both themes are designed, follow the system and can be switched. An agent row messages its claimant (`POST /api/tasks/T1/message`, as `msg`) and stops it at its spend (`POST /api/tasks/T1/budget`, as `task update --budget-tokens`). Runaway rules (`lib/runaway.js`) are shared by `status` (`runaway:` lines, JSON `runaways`) and the board. The human bench (`test/bench/`, docs/human-bench.md) measures the owner's jobs before and after with real browser input. An open board ages live usage on its own: past its stale limit a reading is drawn stale on the agent row and in Spend without a state write, and serve redraws the queue and status sentence.
