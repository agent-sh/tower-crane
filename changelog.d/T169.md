- A harness login failure, such as an expired Claude OAuth session, pauses that harness. It is read from the error records of Claude, Codex, agy, OpenCode and pi. Dispatch routes around it to a fallback on another harness, its exits are not task attempts, and the owner gets one notice with the re-login command. `spawn --wait` exits 1 when a login failure ends the dispatch. A passing login check (one tiny request on Claude and Codex) or a clean probe spawn on the other harnesses clears the pause. A worker that started before the outage cannot clear it.
