- The worker dispatch lock keeps a live holder on the same host and pid namespace whatever its age, also on macOS and Windows, where markers carry no start time. A dispatch that ran past 60 s there could lose the lock to a second dispatch, which then took a slot ranked for another task. A holder elsewhere counts as gone 60 s after its marker last changed; holders touch it every 10 s, also while they wait synchronously for the worktree or state lock.
- The `wait` host no longer waits for the dispatch lock, for a refill or for a climb its recovery dispatches. A held lock defers the work to the next pass, so the host keeps handling events and drains a signal at once instead of freezing for up to 140 s per pass. A climb stays pending until then, or until `recover`.
- A CLI `spawn` and a spawn supervisor still wait up to 140 s for the lock, without blocking the event loop.
- A dispatch lock whose gone holder's marker cannot be removed is reported once per wait, not on every pass. `TOWER_CRANE_LOCK_TRACE` records dispatch lock holdings again, with the label `dispatch`.
