- The worker dispatch lock keeps a live holder on the same host and pid namespace whatever its age, also on macOS and Windows, where markers carry no start time. A dispatch that ran past 60 s there could lose the lock to a second dispatch, which then took a slot ranked for another task. A holder elsewhere counts as gone 60 s after its marker last changed and touches it every 10 s.
- The `wait` host no longer waits for the dispatch lock. A refill that finds another dispatch holding it is deferred to the next pass, so the host keeps handling events and drains a signal at once instead of freezing for up to 140 s per pass.
- A CLI `spawn` still waits up to 140 s for the lock, without blocking its event loop.
