- `tower-crane metrics` splits reworks three ways: tc_caused (certain Tower Crane, host or base causes), unattributed (`cross_task` and head checks without a culprit, which no longer count as Tower Crane's) and task_caused, each with its count and share in text, `--json` and `--by day`.
- `check ci` records `ci_started_at` and `ci_completed_at` from the judged GitHub check runs, and `metrics` reports the CI wait as its own `ci_waiting` lead-time phase, apart from `waiting_for_gates`. The waiting share lists each waiting phase, and the output counts the merged tasks whose older CI events carry no timing.
