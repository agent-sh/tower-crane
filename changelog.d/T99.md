- Merge queue: accepted PRs merge in acceptance order, and only the head of the line runs anything. A stack is one entry ordered by its lowest task, and its check runs on the whole chain merged with main, never on a lower branch. A base that moves during the check, or a head, entry or setting that changes while it runs, gets a new check before any merge or rework; a head sent to rework from inside the reaction lets the PRs behind it move on; and the queue's `done` event names the task that blocks the line and why. It runs the pinned full suite once on its merge with the current base, and only when its head does not already contain that base; a conflicting head goes to rework with its files. Gate evidence stays bound to the submitted sha, so a base move reruns no gate, and merging main into a PR to refresh it is never needed. `merge queue` and `head check` events record the executor and each check.
