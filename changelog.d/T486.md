- On Windows, a contained agent's watchdog no longer takes another process's orphans into the agent's tree. Windows keeps an exited parent's pid on its children and gives that pid to new processes, so a process in the agent's tree that got the pid of an exited dispatch counted that dispatch's detached supervisor, and its agent, against the agent's resource limits, and a resource stop, or an exit when that supervisor belonged to another project's state, stopped them. Nothing recorded that stop: no further live usage, no budget stop, no exit. A child now counts only if it started no earlier than the parent it names.
- Tests: the live-spend fixture traces the supervisor's usage reads, state writes, child exits, watchdog stops and slow process snapshots, and an aborted wait prints the trace's end. The test where a live sample crosses the budget after submit fails at once, naming the fixture's state, when the supervisor reaches its final read before a sample stops the budget, instead of holding until the runner's timeout. `node test/fixtures/live-spend-round.js` runs one stress round: twelve copies of the live-spend file at once.
