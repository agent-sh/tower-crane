- Orchestrator identity recognizes `bash --login`, `--posix --login`, `--interactive` and shells with a terminal on stdin as session shells, and treats any `-c` shell as per-command. Windows command lines are split by CommandLineToArgvW rules, so escaped quotes stay inside their argument. The Windows ancestor walk stops at a parent created after its child, so a reused pid cannot join the chain. The lease docs say in-process subagents share the orchestrator's identity and only `--agent` separates them.
