- The governor's CPU lines (CPU pressure, saturated load, and load without CPU weights) defer work only while under 20% of the CPU sat idle over the last ten seconds, measured from `/proc/stat`. Tasks pinned to a few CPUs or held by a quota no longer defer Tower Crane's work while the other CPUs sit idle: on 2026-10-24, 12 busy loops pinned to 4 of 24 CPUs read as CPU pressure 88% and load 44 to 50 with the CPU 65% idle. `status` shows the idle figure (`idle N% over S s`, JSON `reading.idle`), and a CPU reason names it.
