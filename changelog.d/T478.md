- The orchestrator can answer escalated technical questions without another owner restriction, recorded as `orchestrator-escalated-question` in decisions and status. Answers apply no settings; widening still needs a separate owner approval request. Explicit owner reasons and non-technical kinds retain their owner restriction. Approval requests stay owner-only. Set `decision_delegation.orchestrator_escalated_questions` to false to restore the old refusal.
- The orchestrator can withdraw another agent's open question under the same delegation rule with a required reason. Approval requests keep the asker/owner withdrawal rule.
- Generated budget-stop questions record their spend kind and retain that owner restriction under question delegation. Question reuse preserves the kind and keeps approval requests separate.
