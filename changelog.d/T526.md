- A sandboxed codex agent's `config.toml` sets a `shell_environment_policy` that excludes `AWS_*` names and names containing `SECRET`, `TOKEN`, `PASSWORD`, `PASSWD`, `PASSPHRASE` or `CREDENTIAL`, without regard to case. It also excludes names containing `KEY` unless that occurrence is followed by an underscore and a digit. This exception applies to any name, including `FOO_KEY_1X`, not just git's `GIT_CONFIG_KEY_<n>` entries; another matching word or KEY occurrence still excludes the name. The policy adds names from the user's codex `.env`, except reserved home, path, temp, Git, XDG and Tower Crane names. Those exceptions do not bypass the credential-name patterns. The policy filters names, not values, and leaves codex's own environment unchanged.
- A sandboxed claude agent's commands hide the host's `/tmp` on Linux unless an outside readable path or a retained caller temp directory contains `/tmp`. The sandbox reopens outside `agentReads` paths and retained caller temp directories there, PATH entries there with their install prefixes, and claude's own temp paths. These grants can expose directories beyond the agent's own temp paths. A retained caller `TMPDIR` of `/tmp` keeps `/tmp` visible for claude's proxy sockets.
