- A sandboxed codex agent's `config.toml` sets a `shell_environment_policy` that excludes `AWS_*` names and names containing `KEY`, `SECRET`, `TOKEN`, `PASSWORD`, `PASSWD`, `PASSPHRASE` or `CREDENTIAL`, without regard to case. The policy adds names from the user's codex `.env`, except reserved home, path, temp, Git, XDG and Tower Crane names. Those exceptions do not bypass the credential-name patterns. The policy filters names, not values, and leaves codex's own environment unchanged.
- A sandboxed claude agent's commands hide the host's `/tmp` on Linux. The sandbox reopens the agent's read paths outside the home that lie in `/tmp`, a retained caller temp directory there, PATH entries there with their install prefixes, and claude's own temp paths.
