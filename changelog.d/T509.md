- Gate priority belongs to the task and survives later notifications, rework and resubmission until acceptance, cancellation or `gates prioritize ID --clear`. Hotfix cancellation and supersession also retire priority, so reopening cannot restore it.
- Prioritized tasks run in first-request order. Repeating a request keeps its place; `--rank N` overrides the rank, with lower ranks first and ties in first-request order. Accepted merges keep precedence.
