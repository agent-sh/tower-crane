- Gate commands' stdout and stderr are joined by whole lines in the tests, clean and local CI gates. A stderr chunk can no longer land inside a stdout line that a block-buffered command flushed mid-line, so a secret it cut in two is still redacted in `output.log`, the tests and local CI tails and the summaries. Unfinished last lines end on their own lines, and joining a long line without newlines takes linear time.
- A kept tail that drops earlier output starts at a line, and a line longer than the kept tail is replaced by `[overlong line omitted]` instead of being cut mid-secret. The timeout snapshot keeps only whole lines.
- The clean gate redacts its tool's output before its failure summary cuts it to 20 lines or 200 characters.
