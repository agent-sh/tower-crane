- A resume after a failed start moves the earlier start's attempt log aside when the recorded spawn's supervisor recorded its end, whatever process now has its pids. Before, on a busy Windows host, a reused pid whose creation time PowerShell did not read in time, or that was given out after the read, counted as the earlier attempt still running, and the resume was refused with `EEXIST: log already exists`.
- A failed `stack sync` names the gh step that failed and its reason on stderr. Before, it printed only its `aborted` outcome on stdout and kept the reason in the event log.
