- A re-review after a failed review runs at or above that review's rung also when it falls back to a full review: after an amended or rebased rework, or when the reviewed commit is gone. Before, only a narrowed re-review kept the rung, so an amended rework after a hard failure could get an easy reviewer.
- A fallback full re-review packet names the failed review with its summary, comment, findings and rework request, and asks the reviewer to confirm each prior blocker. Both packet kinds now list the failed review's recorded findings, and the review `spawn` event's `review_scope` records them as `findings`.
