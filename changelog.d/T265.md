- A re-review after a failed review runs at or above that review's rung also when it falls back to a full review: after an amended or rebased rework, or when the reviewed commit is gone. Before, only a narrowed re-review kept the rung, so an amended rework after a hard failure could get an easy reviewer. With `min_rung: "review"` a re-review after rework still starts on the review rung, as before.
- A fallback full re-review packet names the failed review with its summary, comment, findings and rework request, and asks the reviewer to confirm each prior blocker. Both packet kinds now list the failed review's recorded findings, and the review `spawn` event's `review_scope` records them as `findings`.
- The review `spawn` event records `review_floor`: the failed review's SHA, revision, agent, rung and level that set the re-review's rung floor. A fallback full re-review keeps `review_scope: null`, so this field is how the log shows its floor.
