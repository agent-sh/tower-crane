- The host governor no longer keeps room for other sessions out of Tower Crane's own memory. A sample of other sessions takes the level's use as the lower of the tick's first reading and one taken after the units were measured, so a unit that ended meanwhile is not sampled as another session. Room stays kept for memory other sessions let go of while running units grew into it.
- A crossed growth window raises the reservation only for the units of its own set. Units admitted since, in this project or another, keep the ratio in force before it, instead of the crossed ratio times every unit's room.
- A unit in a scope of its own also counts its processes outside the scope: an agent's supervisor, its state broker and the waits the broker runs for the agent. They were sampled as other sessions, and kept as other sessions' room after the unit ended. Each byte counts once, in the innermost unit scope that holds it.
