- A unit that ends during a governor tick is no longer sampled as another session. A sample of other sessions takes the level's use as the lower of the tick's first reading and one taken after the units were measured. Room stays kept for memory other sessions let go of while running units grew into it. A process its unit has not recorded yet, such as a supervisor whose bind write is still queued, can still count as another session's.
- A crossed growth window raises the reservation only for the units of its own set. Units admitted since, in this project or another, keep the ratio in force before it, instead of the crossed ratio times every unit's room.
- A unit in a scope of its own also counts its processes outside the scope while it is registered: an agent's supervisor, its state broker and the waits the broker runs for the agent. Each byte counts once, in the innermost unit scope that holds it. The supervisor removes its unit before its cleanup and gate reactions, so from then until it exits its memory is still sampled as other sessions', and its exit still leaves that much room for them, as before.
