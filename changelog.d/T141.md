- `gates prioritize ID --reason R` (operational: the orchestrator or the owner) moves a task's queued gate reactions ahead of older queued submitted work. Priority applies to later notifications and survives rework and resubmission until acceptance, cancellation or `--clear`. Setting priority for a task with nothing queued is refused. Each request logs a `gates prioritize` event with its reason.
- `status` and `inbox` show the gate queue: running tasks, then queued tasks in the order executors take them, with any prioritized task marked with its reason. A task whose follow-up waits behind its own running reaction is listed as blocked after the queue, since no free executor takes it yet. A reaction that is running is not queued work. `status --json` and `inbox --json` carry the queue as `gate_queue` with `running`, `queued` and `blocked`.
