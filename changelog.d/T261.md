- `TOWER_CRANE_VIA=automation` in the environment no longer lets a second orchestrator session write under another session's live lease. In-process reactions mark their own context, and the supervisor and hook bridge prove engine work with a per-home key from the dispatched agent's home. A claim without a matching key runs as a native command.
- Spawned agents no longer inherit the dispatcher's `TOWER_CRANE_VIA` or its harness session ids (`CLAUDE_SESSION_ID`, `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID`, `CODEX_THREAD_ID`, `CODEX_SESSION_ID`).
