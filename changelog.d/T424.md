- Watchdog supervisors and keepers tolerate two consecutive failed process snapshots and stop on the third with `resource-monitor-failed`. A successful snapshot resets the count. Windows reuses one PowerShell sampler and replaces it after exit or timeout.
- Watchdog identity maps discard exited and reused pids. Cgroup keepers poll population and member files, including nested groups, instead of scanning `/proc` on each tick.
