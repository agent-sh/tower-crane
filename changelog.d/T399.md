- The shell loop rule in the wait lint reads `$'...'` with its escaped quotes, keeps a `#` glued to `$(...)` or `$((...))` as part of the word, and no longer takes `<<` in arithmetic or a `<<<` here-string as a heredoc, so none of them hides a loop. `$((cmd) )` is a subshell in a substitution, not arithmetic. Deeply nested unclosed quotes and substitutions no longer overflow the stack, and many unclosed heredocs are read in linear time.
