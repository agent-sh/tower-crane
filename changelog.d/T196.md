- `wait` drains its active gate on SIGTERM or SIGINT, stops taking new gate work,
  releases its executor lease and exits 0. A second signal kills the active gate
  command tree or cancels its source-page fetch. Interrupted reactions remain
  retryable at the next startup. A final stop notice reports the signal and
  the number of gates drained.
  PR inspection and non-shell Git/GitHub gate commands also remain cancellable
  while running, without blocking signal handlers.
