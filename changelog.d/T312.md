- Worker dispatches take slots one at a time under a dispatch lock in the state directory (`dispatch/lock`). Two refills could race: one prepared the first ready task's worktree while the other found it unfinished, skipped it and gave the slot to a task ranked below it. The worker limit itself held; the order did not. The `refills after exit` automation test failed this way under load.
- Stack refreshes run before the dispatch lock, and so do a new worktree's base and own-head fetches once the task passes the readiness check, for tasks without dependencies. A dependent task fetches its base under the lock, so a dependency that merges during the wait is in it. A spawn waits up to 140 s for the lock, the sum of the waits a holder can make inside it. A spawn that still finds it held fails with exit 3, "worker dispatch is locked by ...". `spawn --ready` stops its batch at that task, and the wait host's refill retries on its next pass instead of caching the failure.
