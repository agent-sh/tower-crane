- `wait` and the board's SSE stream no longer replay restored history when a restore takes more than one read: `cp backup events.jsonl`, which truncates and then writes, or moving the log away and copying it back. After a reset, a record written no later than the newest event the reader had seen, or than its start, is history; it stays queryable but does not wake. The first newer record wakes as usual, including one that lands in the same read as the reset.
