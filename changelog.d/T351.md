- An OSC, DCS, SOS, PM or APC string without its terminator now ends at the next newline or ESC. Before, local CI scanned to the next BEL or ST, which could remove real output between them and took quadratic time over many such strings.
- The tests gate and its timeout report remove the same terminal escapes as local CI, OSC hyperlinks included, before naming failures and redacting. Before, they removed CSI sequences only.
- A local CI artifact that is not valid UTF-8 is binary and kept as written. Before, it was decoded and its invalid bytes became U+FFFD. `docs/state.md` notes that binary artifacts are unredacted and that the orchestrator can read them.
- The tmp-root test runs its default-root gate on fixture roots only. Before, a deep fixture cache made it fall back to the account's real `~/.cache/tc/g`, where the gate wrote and swept.
