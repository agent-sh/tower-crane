- The owner sets a sandbox grant policy once with `project set --sandbox-policy`: read and write paths the orchestrator may add to one task's sandbox (`task update --sandbox`) or a rung's (`ladder set --sandbox`) without asking. Setting or widening the policy stays the owner's; the orchestrator may narrow or clear it.
- Credential stores, network hosts, the session bus and sockets such as `/run/docker.sock` stay owner-only whatever the policy lists. Anything outside the policy opens the owner's decision as before.
- Each grant records a `sandbox grant` event and `sandbox` evidence on the task with who granted it, the policy version and the items; `task show` and `status` list a task's widenings. Narrowing the policy revokes grants it no longer covers, and every dispatch checks them again.
- A worker's `ask --kind credential --setting sandbox --sandbox JSON` for items inside the policy goes to the orchestrator.
