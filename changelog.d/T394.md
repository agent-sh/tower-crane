- The shell loop rule in the wait lint skips quoted text, comments and heredoc bodies when it looks for loop words. A `done` in `echo "(step) done"`, a heredoc line or a comment no longer ends a counted loop early, and `grep -q "(x) for"` in a header no longer opens a loop. A quoted string or heredoc that holds a whole loop, such as `sh -c '...'`, is read as a script of its own.
