- The host governor reserves what running work actually grows to together. Once a minute it opens a growth window with the units running then and follows their summed measured size for `growth_window_min` (60); a closed window's ratio is what the set grew over the room it had. Once `growth_samples_min` (20) windows exist, the budget's growth is the 99th percentile of those ratios times the running units' summed room, never below the largest unit's own room, in place of the sum of every unit's remaining peak; until then it stays the sum. A window that grew past the reservation in force raises the ratio to at least its own for the next `growth_window_min`, under the sum rule as under measured growth. Closed windows, with their prediction, their growth, their ratio and their level's peak, go to a rolling log, `HOST.windows.jsonl`, beside the registry. `status` names the rule, the ratio and the sample count. Use still counts anonymous and shared memory and kernel memory, never page cache, so a finished scope that still holds cached files counts for nothing.
