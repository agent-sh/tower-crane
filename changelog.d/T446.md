- The host governor reserves what running work actually grows to together. Once a minute it opens a growth window with the units running then and follows their summed measured size for `growth_window_min` (60). Once `growth_samples_min` (20) windows of the same mix of kinds exist, the budget's growth is the 99th percentile of the growth those windows saw, in place of the sum of every unit's remaining peak; until then it stays the sum. A window that grew past the reservation in force raises the next one for its mix. Closed windows, with their prediction, their growth and the binding level's peak, go to a rolling log, `HOST.windows.jsonl`, beside the registry. `status` names the rule, the mix and the sample count. Use still counts anonymous and shared memory and kernel memory, never page cache, so a finished scope that still holds cached files counts for nothing.
