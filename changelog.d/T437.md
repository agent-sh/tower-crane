- On Windows, containment waits for a slow CIM process snapshot and stop as long as other Tower Crane snapshots do, instead of failing the attempt with `resource-monitor-failed` after 2 seconds.
- On Windows, a contained command that exits before its first process snapshot still has the children it started found, stopped or retained, and a gate command's leftover processes are stopped from a final snapshot.
- A caller outside its systemd user manager, such as a hosted CI runner's service, gets its scope in the manager's slice of the same name; the containment tests assert that placement there.
