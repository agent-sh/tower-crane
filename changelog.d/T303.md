- Readers without the state lock now see a commit as soon as its events are in the log. Between a commit's append and its renames, and after its holder died there, a reader reads the commit's staged files in place of the files they replace. It reads them only when their id is the last complete line of the log, so it never shows a commit that did not reach its commit point. Before, the `wait` host could judge a fresh `submit` by the old `tasks.json` and drop its reaction until the next `wait` restart.
- The escalation test that asserts no climb after a superseded review failure now waits for the monitor to exit, so a later climb cannot slip past it.
